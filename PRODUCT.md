# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: plain static HTML/CSS (no build step). Chosen so the folder can be dragged onto Netlify and students can edit text without tooling. Deployed on Netlify; the existing free WordPress.com site (bcpscioly-nevda.wordpress.com) stays up and links to it.

## Users

- Other Science Olympiad teams, coaches and students curious about the BASIS Cedar Park team.
- Visiting coaches registering teams for the BCP Winter Invitational.

## Product Purpose

The "unofficial" website of the BASIS Cedar Park Science Olympiad team: show who the team is and what it has achieved, and send coaches to register for the invitational. Success = a visiting coach finds the invitational date and registration link in seconds.

## Operating Context

- Content is maintained by students; the old WordPress site is the source for all facts.
- Registration happens on an external Smartsheet form; the site has no backend.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pages carried over from the old site: Home, 2027 Invitational, About Us, Division B, Contact (mailto).
- About Us has no content yet ("Under construction"); do not invent history, coaches or stats.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: BASIS Cedar Park Science Olympiad (BCP Scioly). Tagline: "Unofficial" BCP Scioly website.
- Team colour green (logo and team shirts). Logo: `site/images/invitational.png`.
- User-pinned direction: plain, newspaper-like typography and layout.

## Evidence on Hand

- 2027 BCP Winter Invitational: 2nd annual, in person, Jan. 9, 2027, new this year Division B and Division C. Registration: https://app.smartsheet.com/b/publish?EQBCT=cf9c8fb5611f40f4a862bc36cc675d06
- Results: 2024/2025 Division B 4th place at Nationals (Nebraska); 2024/2025 Division C State Team.
- Photos: `site/images/state-div-c-2025.jpg`, `site/images/nationals-div-b-2025.jpg`.
- Contact: bcpscioly@gmail.com
- Absent: venue/address, schedule, event list, fees, roster names, coach names, team history. Never fabricate.

## Product Principles

- Facts from the team only; gaps stay visible as "coming soon", not filled with invented copy.
- Registration is the one action that matters.
- Easy for students to edit by hand.
