BCP Winter Invitational returns Jan. 9

Our 2nd annual in-person tournament opens to Division B and Division C

Welcome to the 2027 BCP Winter Invitational, our second annual in-person tournament for the 2026/2027 Science Olympiad season.

New this year: the invitational is open to both Division B and Division C teams.

The tournament will be held in person on Saturday, January 9, 2027. Coaches can register their teams through the registration form.

For questions about the tournament, email bcpscioly@gmail.com.
